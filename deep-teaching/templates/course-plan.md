## Teaching strategy

Use this progression:

1. Activate the prerequisites.
2. Introduce one complete mental model.
3. Demonstrate the mechanism with a concrete example or figure.
4. Ask the learner to predict before revealing the result.
5. Provide guided practice with staged hints.
6. Require independent performance that matches the objective.
7. Test transfer in a different context.
8. Revisit important objectives after a delay.
9. Interleave related concepts after separate instruction.
10. Adapt the next lesson from recorded evidence.

## Adaptation rules

Use the following evidence-based decisions:

| Evidence | Next action |
|---|---|
| Independent success that matches the objective | Continue or increase difficulty |
| Correct answer with low confidence | Ask for an explanation and schedule retrieval |
| Correct recognition without successful application | Add production or transfer practice |
| Repeated conceptual error | Add another representation and targeted practice |
| Missing prerequisite | Pause the sequence and repair the prerequisite |
| Success only after strong hints | Repeat with reduced scaffolding |
| Independent transfer success | Advance the objective state |
| Strong immediate performance without delayed evidence | Schedule retrieval; do not mark retained |
